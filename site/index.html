Find the ISS asks a free online tracker, wheretheiss.at, where the International Space Station is right now, and points the ring at it. It asks once when this view opens, and again each time you press the button. It is the only part of the sky that needs the internet: the stars, planets and Moon are all worked out on your phone, so with no signal everything except the space station still works.
